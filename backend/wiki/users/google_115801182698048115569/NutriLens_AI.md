### Overview
NutriLens AI is an AI-powered personalized nutrition and food health intelligence platform that Jayashan is building under the Ascentic AI Launch Pad mentorship program. It enables users to scan food packages and meals, analyze ingredients and nutrition labels using OCR and LLMs, receive personalized health recommendations, discover healthier alternatives, and track long-term nutrition habits through an intelligent AI assistant.
