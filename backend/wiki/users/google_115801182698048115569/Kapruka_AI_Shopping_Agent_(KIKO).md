### Overview
KIKO (Kapruka Shopping Agent) is an advanced, production-grade conversational commerce engine built specifically for Kapruka, Sri Lanka's leading e-commerce platform. It converts traditional e-commerce browsing into a fluid, multi-agent conversational experience, enabling users to search for products, check stock, validate delivery availability across Sri Lankan cities, manage shopping carts, build order summaries, execute guest checkouts, and track live order status—all through natural dialogue in 5 supported language formats.

### Known Facts & Data
