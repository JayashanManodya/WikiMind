## Overview
Tesla, Inc. is the world's most recognized electric vehicle manufacturer, founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning. Under the leadership of Elon Musk, Tesla has revolutionized the automotive industry by popularizing electric vehicles (EVs) globally. The company is known for its innovative approach to electric mobility, focusing on advanced battery technology, autonomous driving features, and sustainable manufacturing practices. Tesla's product lineup includes several popular models such as the Model S, Model 3, Model X, Model Y, and the upcoming Cybertruck. The company has established a strong brand reputation and is a global leader in EV sales, continuously pushing the boundaries of technology with features like Autopilot and a vast Supercharger network.
