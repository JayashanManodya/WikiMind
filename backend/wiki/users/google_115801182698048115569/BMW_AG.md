## Overview
BMW AG is a leading luxury automobile manufacturer that combines luxury with electric mobility. The company offers premium electric vehicles that emphasize comfort, performance, and sustainability. BMW's electric lineup includes models such as the i4, iX, i5, and i7, which are known for their luxury interiors, powerful electric motors, and advanced infotainment systems. With a strong reputation in the luxury market and a commitment to high-quality engineering, BMW provides an exceptional driving experience while maintaining excellent safety features.
