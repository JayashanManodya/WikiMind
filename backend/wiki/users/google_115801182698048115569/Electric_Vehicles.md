# Electric Vehicles

**Entity Type**: `CONCEPT`  
**Last Updated**: `2026-07-29`

## Overview
Electric vehicles (EVs) are automobiles that are propelled by electric motors, using energy typically stored in rechargeable batteries. The electric vehicle industry encompasses a wide range of vehicles, including passenger cars, buses, and trucks, and is characterized by advancements in battery technology, autonomous driving features, and sustainable manufacturing practices. The growth of the EV market is driven by increasing consumer demand for environmentally friendly transportation, government support, and technological innovations.
