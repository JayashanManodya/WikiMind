### Overview
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a framework that combines retrieval of relevant documents with generative models to produce answers based on the retrieved information.

### Known Facts & Data
- The multi-agent pipeline consists of four stages: Planning, Retrieval, Summarization, and Verification. (Provenance: 5.2 Multi-Agent Pipeline)
