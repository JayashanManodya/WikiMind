## Overview
Rivian Automotive, Inc. specializes in electric pickup trucks and SUVs designed for adventure and outdoor activities. The company is known for its innovative vehicle design and strong performance, offering models such as the R1T, R1S, and Electric Delivery Van (EDV). Rivian's vehicles are characterized by off-road capability, large battery packs, and premium interiors, making them ideal for adventure enthusiasts. The company's focus on commercial partnerships further enhances its position in the electric vehicle market.
