## Overview
Nissan Motor Corporation is a pioneer in the electric vehicle market, having introduced the mass-market Nissan Leaf in 2010. The company has established a strong global presence with reliable and affordable electric vehicles. Nissan's electric lineup includes models such as the Leaf and Ariya, which are known for their comfortable driving experience and easy maintenance. With a focus on providing good value for everyday drivers, Nissan continues to innovate in the electric vehicle space.
