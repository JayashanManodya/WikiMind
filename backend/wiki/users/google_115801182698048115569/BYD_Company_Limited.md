# BYD Company Limited

**Entity Type**: `ORGANIZATION`  
**Last Updated**: `2026-07-29`

## Overview
BYD Company Limited, which stands for Build Your Dreams, is one of the largest electric vehicle manufacturers in the world, founded in 1995. Initially focused on producing rechargeable batteries, BYD has expanded its operations to include electric cars, buses, and trucks. The company is recognized for its Blade Battery technology, which enhances safety and longevity. BYD's product offerings include popular models such as the Seal, Dolphin, Atto 3, Han, and Tang. With a strong presence in Asia, Europe, and Latin America, BYD is committed to providing affordable electric vehicles while maintaining high safety standards and producing its own batteries.
