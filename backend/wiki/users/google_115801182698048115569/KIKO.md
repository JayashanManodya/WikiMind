### Overview
KIKO is an AI-powered conversational shopping assistant developed by Jayashan, featuring a 5-agent architecture using the Kapruka MCP. It allows users to browse categories, search products, view product details, validate delivery, manage carts, complete guest checkout, and track orders through natural language conversations in English, Sinhala, and Tamil.
