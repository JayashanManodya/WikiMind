## Overview
NIO Inc. is a Chinese electric vehicle company recognized for its luxury electric SUVs and innovative battery-swapping technology. The company focuses on providing high-performance electric vehicles designed for a premium experience. NIO's product lineup includes models such as the ET5, ET7, ES6, and ES8. With a commitment to innovation and customer satisfaction, NIO is making significant strides in the electric vehicle market, particularly in the luxury segment.
