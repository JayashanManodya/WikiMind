**Entity Type**: `ORGANIZATION`

## Overview
Mercedes-Benz is a renowned luxury automobile manufacturer that produces electric vehicles under its EQ brand. The company is known for its premium quality, advanced technology, and exceptional comfort. Mercedes-Benz's electric vehicle offerings include models such as the EQS, EQE, EQA, EQB, and EQS SUV. These vehicles feature luxury interiors, long battery ranges, and advanced digital displays, along with intelligent driving assistance systems. With a strong global brand recognition, Mercedes-Benz continues to lead in the luxury electric vehicle market.
