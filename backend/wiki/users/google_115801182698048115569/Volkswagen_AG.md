**Entity Type**: `ORGANIZATION`

## Overview
Volkswagen AG is one of the largest automobile manufacturers globally, with significant investments in electric vehicle development, particularly through its ID series. The company has a rich history in the automotive industry and is known for its commitment to innovation and sustainability. Volkswagen's electric vehicle lineup includes models such as the ID.3, ID.4, and ID. Buzz, as well as the Audi Q4 e-tron. The company utilizes a modern electric platform (MEB) to enhance the driving experience, offering comfortable interiors and advanced safety systems. Volkswagen's extensive global manufacturing network and strong research and development capabilities position it as a key player in the electric vehicle market.
